My name is Qi-Hang Sun, and I am a Ph.D. candidate at [Beihang University](https://www.buaa.edu.cn/) under the supervision of Prof. [Ren-Yong Guo](https://sem.buaa.edu.cn/info/1363/16307.htm). I am currently visiting the research group of Prof. [Tobias Harks](https://www.fim.uni-passau.de/en/mathopt) at University of Passau.

My current research focuses on reservation-based travel demand management (TDM) strategies. I model commuters' noncompliance behavior and analyze the resulting equilibria and long-term interactions between system operators and strategic participants. I am also interested in mechanism design for congestion management and hope to explore this direction in future research.

### Research interests

- Travel demand management (TDM) strategies
- Pedestrian dynamics
- Low-altitude urban logistics

My methodological interests include optimization in transportation.

I graduated from Beihang University in 2023 with a Bachelor's degree in Management (Information Management and Information System) and a minor Bachelor's degree in Engineering (Biomedical Engineering).
